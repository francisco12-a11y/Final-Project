## Lead magnet
**Title (MAGIC formula):** *The Founder Delegation Audit: Find the 15 Hours a Week You're Doing a $10/Hour Job*

- **Format:** PDF worksheet + scoring guide (delivered by email instantly after opt-in).
- **Promise (one specific problem):** A founder can't see which tasks to hand off first. In 20 minutes, the audit produces a ranked list of tasks to delegate, the hours recoverable per week, and a script for the first handoff conversation.
- **MAGIC check:** Measurable (hour + dollar value per task), Actionable (ranked handoff list + script), Goal-driven (buy back 10–15 hrs/wk), Interested audience (founders drowning in ops), Credible (uses Pareto's own 4.3x ROI math and pain-point research).
- **Value Equation:** High certainty (a worksheet, not theory — you finish it with an answer), high value (15 hrs/wk ≈ their hiring fee pays back in weeks), low time investment (20 min), low effort (checklist + math done for them).

## Qualification criteria
A lead is **qualified** when ALL are true:
1. Has a fundable/scaling business (running paid ads or active clients — not an idea stage).
2. Currently spends 15+ hrs/week on admin/ops tasks (inbox, scheduling, CRM, data entry, customer follow-ups).
3. Has revenue (≥ $10k/mo) — can afford to hire vs. "someday."
4. Directly involved in hiring decisions (owner/co-founder).

Form questions map 1:1 to these; score ≥ 3 → qualified → booking page. Otherwise → thank-you page + nurture.

## ICP (one paragraph)
Pareto's ICP is a founder or owner-operator of a growing service or e-commerce business doing roughly $10k–$100k/mo. They've proven the model but became the bottleneck: they run the inbox, the calendar, the CRM, the follow-ups — 10–15 hours a week of $10/hour work inside a $100/hour brain. They've tried VAs and failed (hiring, training, quality), and they're skeptical of cheap offshore help. They don't want another course or tool; they want a trained operator who shows up, owns systems, and lets them do founder work. Pareto's language: *"You don't have a people problem. You have a delegation problem."*

## Founder personas (by cause, in client words)
1. **"Drowning Operator" Dan** — cause: success. Revenue grew, ops grew with it; he's the ceiling. *"I'm doing $10/hour work in a $100/hour job. I'm the bottleneck of my own company."* Angle: time recovery / ROI.
2. **"Failed-VA" Vanessa** — cause: burned. Hired a cheap VA twice; spent more time managing than saving. *"I spent more time training my VA than I got back. Never again… unless someone else did the vetting."* Angle: done-for-you hiring, top 1% vetting.
3. **"Chaos-at-Scale" Chris** — cause: no systems. Everything lives in his head/DMs; team can't move without him. *"If I take a week off, the business stops."* Angle: systems + AI-powered operators.
4. **"Solo-until-Now" Sofia** — cause: bootstrap pride. Did everything herself to protect margin; now growth stalled. *"I didn't leave my job to become my own assistant."* Angle: permission + leverage narrative.

## The next-step offer

The audit's output (their delegation list) is literally the job description for their Right Hand — the natural next step is "have Pareto find the person who takes these tasks off your plate."
