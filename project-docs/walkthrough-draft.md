### Part 1 · Mini Second Brain and market research (L1, L2)

My Second Brain (Obsidian) holds everything the funnel claims: The Hire book (all seven steps and the seven principles), the full Bootcamp BC7 recordings and notes (days 1–9), Pareto's public pages (site, bootcamp site, Wall of Love), the hiring-regions chapter, and live teardowns of four competitors.

**Three competitor lead magnets, verified on their live sites:**

- **Athena — "Delegation Assessment" quiz.** Format: 10 questions, email gated at the end. Promise: your named delegation level and your specific barrier. For: overwhelmed founders, including EA skeptics. Qualification: answers score into six levels, Beginner to Mastery. After opt-in: an instant diagnosis whose top three outcomes all say the ceiling is the match, not the mindset — then a bridge into their match signup.
- **Belay — "Find your MVP" guide.** Format: gated PDF plus a large free library. Promise: talent quality proof ("only 3% of applicants ever become BELAY contractors"). For: leaders comparing providers. Qualification: the highest-friction capture of the three — a phone number with SMS consent, trading volume for intent. After opt-in: download, then SMS and email nurture.
- **Virtual Latinos — "Are you doing too much?" quiz + recruiter call.** Format: site-wide quiz feeding a business survey, then a recruiter call. Promise: real insights, public pricing. For: US businesses hiring LatAm talent, organized by vertical (legal, real estate, admin). Qualification: the verticals self-sort leads before any form. After opt-in: 3–4 candidates to interview, with vertical-matched testimonials.

**The pattern I stole:** the quiz is the category's default magnet, email gates come last, every magnet doubles as a qualifier, and vertical proof converts. My funnel applies all four — with one upgrade: where Athena assigns you a generic level, my test computes *your* hours and *your* money.

**ICP in one paragraph:** US-based founders of growing service businesses who are personally the operational bottleneck — real revenue ($10k+/month at this launch's routing), 15+ hours a week of admin and ops on their own plate, and the hiring decision on their desk. They want a strategic full-time partner, not a task-list VA; they're open to AI; 60%+ of them arrive burned by a previous VA.

**Founder personas, by cause, in their words:**

- *Agency owner at capacity:* "I just can't even imagine not having my EA right now." (Joe Polish) — cause: client comms and scheduling ate the calendar.
- *Real estate operator:* "My executive assistant runs point on critical projects and keeps track of so many endless opportunities." (Andrew Myers) — cause: deals die in slow follow-up.
- *Creator with a content bottleneck:* "I won't even tell her to do it, and she'll just go and figure out how to do it." (Elina Panteleyeva) — cause: ops crushing creation.
- *Law firm owner (flagged as a hypothesis, backed by competitor proof):* "Every missed call is a case walking to another firm." — cause: $400/hour time burned on $20/hour work; Virtual Latinos already runs a whole legal vertical for this demand.

Plus the cross-persona state most leads arrive in: "I've had three different executive assistants before, and I thought it was me… I was burning through them." (Eric Ritter)

### Part 4 · The lead magnet and the offer

**Format:** an interactive test (8 questions, instant result, no email to see the number) plus a delivered systems pack. Why this format: the research showed the quiz is the category's default magnet — but every competitor quiz stops at a generic label. Mine computes the founder's actual recoverable hours, their dollar value at Pareto's own $200/hr founder rate, and their first two handoffs. The output is simultaneously the qualification data and the sales pitch.

**The promise:** find the hours you're losing to the assistant job, and hand them off — starting this week.

**MAGIC title:** *The Founder Delegation Test* + *The Right Hand Starter Kit: the systems, scripts, 30-day plan, and real costs of your first hire.* Measurable (hours and dollars on the spot), Actionable (fill-in Week 1, script, systems), Goal-driven (10–15 hours a week back), Interested audience (founders drowning in ops), Credible (Pareto's own math and guarantees).

**Qualification criteria:** revenue $10k+/month, 15+ hours a week in ops, founder or co-founder and hiring decision-maker, and 10+ recoverable hours found by the test. The form and the test answers both feed these rules; anyone who doesn't qualify is told so honestly and keeps the materials.

**The next-step offer:** a Matching Call — bring the completed kit, because your Week 1 list is your first job description. Backed by Freedom 40 and lifetime replacement.

### Part 7 · CRM and automation (L11, L12)

**Pipeline (6 stages):** New Lead → Qualified → Call Booked → Call Done → Won (Matched) → Nurture (Not Qualified). Unqualified leads are never deleted; they wait in stage 6 with a path back.

**The five workflows:**

- **Opt-in** (form submitted): creates the opportunity, tags qualified or nurture, delivers the kit PDF as an attachment, notifies me on qualified leads.
- **Qualified follow-up** (tag added): waits one day, checks for a booking, sends two touches maximum (day 1 and day 3).
- **Nurture** (tag added): three teach-only emails on days 2, 5, and 8 — delegation lessons from the kit, no pitching.
- **Booking** (appointment booked): confirmation immediately, reminder 24 hours before, calendar filter set to this funnel's calendar.
- **Post-call** (2 hours after the appointment): opportunity moves to Call Done, and the thank-you email goes out.

**Testing:** both form paths tested in incognito on production; the kit's arrival confirmed; the workflow execution logs are screenshotted in the L11/L12 folders — receipts, not claims.
