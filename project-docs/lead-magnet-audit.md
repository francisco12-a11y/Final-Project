## How this works (20 minutes, three steps)

1. **Log** — list every recurring task you did this week (Step 1).
2. **Score** — rate each task on two scales (Step 2).
3. **Rank & hand off** — get your delegation shortlist and your first handoff script (Step 3).

Do it honestly. The math is uncomfortable on purpose.

## Step 2 · Score every task (two questions each)

**Q1 — What would it cost to hire someone to do this?**
- A · Under $15/hour (data entry, scheduling, inbox triage) — **3 points**
- B · $15–50/hour (follow-ups, CRM, reporting) — **2 points**
- C · $50+/hour (strategy, sales, product) — **0 points**

**Q2 — Does this task need *you*, or just need *doing*?**
- A · Anyone trained could do it — **3 points**
- B · Needs judgment, but not my judgment — **2 points**
- C · Genuinely needs me (vision, key relationships) — **0 points**

**Delegation Score = Q1 + Q2.** Max 6 per task.

> If you scored C on Q2 but A on Q1, that's a "systemize first" task — write down the process once, then hand it off.

## Your first handoff conversation (the script nobody gives you)

Most delegation fails at the handoff, not the hiring. Use this:

> "I've audited my week and this task — [TASK] — is now yours. Here's what 'done' looks like: [OUTCOME, NOT METHOD]. You own it end to end. Decision I'm delegating to you: [E.G. REFUNDS UNDER $100]. Check in with me Friday with one question: what did you decide?"

Rules:
- Hand off the **outcome**, not the method. If you script their steps, you've built a $10/hour puppet — and you're still the operator.
- Delegate one **decision authority** with every task. That's what makes it stick.
- Weekly check-in on decisions made, never on tasks done.
